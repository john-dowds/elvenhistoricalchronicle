The Chronicle Side Car

The Chronicle Side Car feature allows you to work on filling out your character’s application side by side with the latest editions of the Chronicle, allowing a good frame of reference when needed.

Some things to note about the feature:

  • It will automatically jump to the appropriate next section based on your answers.
  • You can skip non-essential questions; don't feel compelled to fill out every single one. Just go with what makes sense.
  • The path map will show you your progression (though it is still in beta).

Warning: Clearing your browser cache or history may risk erasing your progress if you haven’t submitted. Be sure you download and save the file if you need to pause! You can always upload it again later and continue from where you left off.

Restart form?

Clicking “Restart form” will wipe your local progress on this device and return the questionnaire to the beginning.
